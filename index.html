Running a B2B collections desk, end to end

Four working projects built around one company: Westline Auto Parts, a four-yard auto parts distributor in California that sells on credit to body shops and garages. Together they cover the daily desk, the month-end close, the causes of disputes, and how the team is trained.

Westline is fictional. Every account, invoice, call and dispute comes from one synthetic simulation, so the numbers agree across all four projects. No real customer data is used.

Standard operating procedure

Month-End AR Reconciliation SOP

How the desk closes the month in six business days. The ledger is tied out, every open invoice is sorted into one of six outcomes, and each list goes to the team that can clear it. It includes roles for six teams, controls and approval limits, and a fully worked September 2026 close.

Root-cause analysis

Chargeback and Refund Root-Cause Map

An analysis of 175 card chargebacks and 430 escalations. Most disputes were refunds the company already owed but had not paid. The map follows a return across six teams, shows where it stalled, and sets out the fixes and the weekly review that keeps them in place.

Read the analysis (PDF)

What it shows

  • 87% of chargebacks traced to six failed hand-offs between teams
  • The evidence that wins genuine disputes, at a 77% win rate
  • Controls against broker orders and impersonation fraud
